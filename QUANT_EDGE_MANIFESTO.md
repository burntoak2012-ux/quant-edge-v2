# The Quant Edge Manifesto

## Football is context, not certainty

A match is never reduced to one label. Quant Edge exists to help people investigate the context around a fixture: team strength, recent form, player availability, projected lineups, market prices, league position, and historical performance.

The product is not a tipping service. It does not tell people what to bet, promise profit, or turn uncertainty into false confidence. It gives users a structured starting point for making their own informed decisions.

## Our principles

### 1. Evidence before excitement

Every view should be grounded in visible data. Ratings, probabilities, odds, lineups, standings, and player histories should be inspectable rather than presented as mysterious authority.

### 2. Probability is not a promise

A 60% model estimate is not a guarantee. It is an estimate that should be compared with the market, the available context, and the user's own judgement.

### 3. Show the disagreement

The useful question is often not “Who wins?” It is “Where do the model, the market, and the available evidence disagree?” Quant Edge highlights that disagreement without disguising it as certainty.

### 4. Make uncertainty visible

Missing lineups, unavailable odds, old baseline data, limited samples, and provider restrictions should be shown clearly. A blank or pending value is better than invented precision.

### 5. Explain the model

Player ratings should reflect meaningful inputs such as performance, minutes, appearances, role, production, and availability. Team ratings should reflect form, results, goals, defensive record, and opponent context as the model develops.

### 6. Measure ourselves

Predictions are snapshots, not proof. We store them, settle them against actual results, and measure accuracy and calibration over time. The product earns trust by showing where it is right, wrong, and uncertain.

### 7. Keep the user in control

Quant Edge supports research. It does not replace judgement, financial planning, or responsible gambling choices. Users decide whether and how to act.

## What Quant Edge is

A football research workspace for comparing:

- team performance
- player and projected lineup ratings
- home, draw, and away probabilities
- bookmaker market context
- league standings and fixture schedules
- career and competition history
- prediction performance over time

## What Quant Edge is not

- a guaranteed winning system
- financial advice
- a promise of betting profit
- a substitute for responsible gambling
- a reason to stake more than someone can afford to lose

## Our promise

We will make the evidence easier to see, the assumptions easier to question, and the uncertainty harder to ignore.
